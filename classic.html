How the work runs (416) 319-8444

Five steps, in the order they happen.

No photographs of work in progress here. A kitchen part way through is not a thing anyone wants to look at, and it is not what you are buying.

01

You send photographs

One wide photograph of each run of cabinet, plus a rough count of doors and drawers. That is usually enough to price the work without anyone coming to the house.

02

A written price comes back

Every line of it, in writing, before anything comes off the wall. If the photographs leave a question open, Everfinish asks it before quoting rather than after.

03

The fronts come off

Doors and drawer fronts are taken off. The cabinet boxes, the counters, the floor and the layout stay exactly where they are.

04

Fronts and boxes are sprayed

Sprayed, not brushed. Fronts and boxes are finished in the colour agreed on the estimate, so the doors and the carcasses read as one kitchen.

05

The kitchen goes back together

Fronts are rehung in the same layout the kitchen started in. You get the room back, not a project.

Colour, hardware and the schedule for your kitchen are settled in writing on the estimate, before you commit to anything.

Questions Request an estimate

What exactly does Everfinish do?

Kitchen cabinet refinishing. Doors, drawer fronts, cabinet boxes and face frames get a new finish. That is the whole business.

Do the cabinet boxes come out?

No. The boxes stay on the wall. So do the counters, the floor and the layout. The doors and the drawer fronts are the only things that leave the room.

Which cities do you cover?

Vaughan, Richmond Hill and Markham, Ontario. If your kitchen sits just outside those three, call and ask.

Do you have to come to the house to price it?

Usually not. Wide photographs of each run of cabinet and a rough door and drawer count are normally enough. If a kitchen needs looking at in person, Everfinish says so instead of guessing.

How is the work priced?

Per kitchen, off the number of doors and drawer fronts and the condition they are in. No two kitchens come out the same, so there is no list price to publish. You get the figure in writing before anything starts.

Who picks the colour?

You do. Send a colour name or a chip with your photographs and it goes on the estimate in writing, so there is no confusion about what gets sprayed.

Is the kitchen torn apart while the work runs?

The boxes, the counters, the floor and the appliances stay in place, so the room is never stripped out. The schedule for your kitchen goes on the estimate.

Can you refinish cabinets that were already finished once?

Send photographs of them. Everfinish looks at what is on there now and tells you what it means for the work, or tells you it is not worth doing.
