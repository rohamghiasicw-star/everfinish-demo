What is deliberately missing

No coating brand, no warranty, no day count, no prices and no review block. Nothing on these pages is invented, so those lines stay out until Leo answers them. They drop straight in afterwards.

Forms are wired and validate, but they do not send anywhere yet. That gets pointed at Leo's inbox and phone on the real build.
